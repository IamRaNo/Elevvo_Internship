## 📌 Project Overview  
The **Chinook database** is a popular sample dataset representing a digital music store.  
In this project, I performed **exploratory SQL analysis** across multiple tables (Customers, Invoices, Tracks, Albums, Artists, Employees, etc.) to uncover **business insights**.  

The repo contains:  
- ✅ **SQL Queries** for both single-table and multi-table analysis  
- ✅ **PDF Report** summarizing insights in a business-friendly format  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
Here are some highlights from the analysis (details in the PDF):  

- 👥 **Customers**  
  - Most customers are from **USA** 🇺🇸  
  - Duplicate checks reveal no major anomalies  

- 💵 **Invoices & Revenue**  
  - **Total Revenue**: Derived from all invoices  
  - **Top Country by Revenue**: USA leads  
  - Clear **monthly & yearly revenue trends**  

- 🎶 **Tracks & Genres**  
  - Rock genre dominates the track list  
  - Track lengths vary widely (shortest under 1 min, longest ~17 min)  

- 🎤 **Artists & Albums**  
  - Certain artists (like **AC/DC**) generate higher revenue  
  - Multiple albums with strong sales performance  

- 🧑‍💼 **Employees**  
  - Identified the **most senior** & **most junior** employees  
  - Employee–Manager reporting relationships mapped  

- 🏆 **Top Performers**  
  - Best-selling track  
  - Top revenue-generating artist  
  - Most valuable customer
